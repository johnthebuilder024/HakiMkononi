{% if lang == "en" %}

We want to be honest with you about what HakiMkononi is and what it is not.

📌
This is legal information, not legal advice

We tell you what the law says. We do not represent you. For court cases or complex matters, you need a real lawyer. NLAS offers free lawyers for those who cannot afford one.

✅
Always verify the sections

We cite real section numbers. You can always confirm them yourself at kenyalaw.org. Laws can change and we may not always have the latest version.

🤖
AI can get things wrong

Our system is very careful but no AI is perfect. Treat the answer as a starting point, not a final verdict. When in doubt, talk to a lawyer.

🔒
Your questions are private

You do not need to sign up. We do not share your questions with anyone. We only store them to improve the service over time.

{% else %}
