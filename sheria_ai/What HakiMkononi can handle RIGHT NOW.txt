What it cannot handle yet — gaps worth filling
1. Copyright & Intellectual Property ← you asked about this Kenya has the Copyright Act (Cap 130) and Industrial Property Act 2001. Common real cases:

Music piracy / someone using your song without permission
Copying software, books, artwork
Trademark infringement (someone using your business name/logo)
Patent disputes
Add: Copyright Act (Cap 130), Industrial Property Act 2001, Trade Marks Act (Cap 506)

2. Housing / Tenancy for residential flats The current Rent Restriction Act (Cap 296) only covers controlled premises. Kenya is passing a new Landlord and Tenant Bill that covers all residential tenancies. Common gap: tenant in a private estate with no controlled tenancy protection.

Add: Factual guidance on this gap since the law is not yet enacted.

3. Banking & Mobile Money Very common questions:

Bank wrongly freezing account
Loan charged wrong interest
Bank refusing to reverse fraud transaction
Mobile money agent fraud
CRB blacklisting without notice
Add: Banking Act (Cap 488), Microfinance Act 2006, National Payment System Act 2011, CRB regulations (Credit Reference Bureau Regulations 2013)

4. Insurance

Insurer refusing to pay accident claim
Medical insurance claim denied
Car insurance claim disputes
Insurer delaying payment unreasonably
Add: Insurance Act (Cap 487), Insurance (Motor Vehicle Third Party Risks) Act

5. Environment / Noise Pollution

Factory polluting a river near your farm
Noise pollution from a bar or industry
Quarry blasting damaging your house
Add: Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA) 1999, Noise Prevention and Control Rules

6. Disability Rights

Employer refusing to hire because of disability
Building with no wheelchair access (public building)
School refusing admission to child with disability
Add: Persons with Disabilities Act 2003, Constitution Article 54

7. Gender & Reproductive Rights

Forced sterilisation
Denial of maternal health services
Gender-based workplace discrimination beyond what Employment Act covers
Add: Health Act 2017, Protection of Health Information regulations

8. Succession / Wills (deeper) Currently Law of Succession Act is loaded but the common practical questions need more:

Challenging a will
Surviving spouse rights
Disinherited child rights
Grant of probate / letters of administration procedure
The Act is there — just needs more intent keywords to catch these queries.

9. Public Procurement

Contractor unpaid by government after completing work
Tender unfairly cancelled
Conflict of interest in award
Add: Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act 2015

10. Entertainment & Sports Contracts

Artist signed unfair record deal
Athlete unpaid by club
Manager taking undisclosed commission
Add: Copyright Act (already covers music), guidance on contract law principles

11. Landlord-Tenant for Land (Caretakers / Squatters)

Caretaker claiming ownership after years of occupation
Adverse possession claims
Absentee landlord vs long-term occupant
This is partly covered by Land Act and Land Registration Act already loaded — just needs better intent keywords for "caretaker", "squatter", "adverse possession".

Priority order for adding
Copyright Act + Industrial Property Act — most asked by musicians, designers, small businesses
Banking Act + CRB Regulations — bank account freezing and loan disputes are extremely common
Insurance Act — car accident claims are daily questions
Persons with Disabilities Act — important gap, Constitution already covers it partly
EMCA (Environment) — pollution near farms is a rural Kenya issue
